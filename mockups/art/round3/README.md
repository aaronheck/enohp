# Yelnats hero art, round 3: Comic Strip (A3)

The files are `concept-A3.html` and three renders: `concept-A3.png` (390×844), `concept-A3-mid.png` (390×700) and `concept-A3-small.png` (375×560), all @2x. `sheet.png` shows all three side by side.

## The panels
Panels 1–3 sit in the top row and panel 4 spans the full width underneath. Butter connector badges (›) link 1 → 2 → 3, and a downward one leads from 3 into 4.

1. **pick a word**: a tomato bean says "pickle jar" (cream pill).
2. **copy it backwards**: the bean's own voice (sky waveform) → ◀◀ → the butter flipped waveform, captioned "your voice, flipped". Below it, the same bean echoes it back in a butter speech bubble with the same backwards waveform.
3. **we rewind**: the butter wave flips across the cream mirror line (◀◀ on it) into the sky wave, captioned with a wobbly "pickle jah?".
4. **friends guess down the line**: three beans, "tickle car?" → "nickel bar?" → "nick's cage!". The last bean is drawn cracking up: eyes squeezed shut, mouth open in a laugh, hand slapped on its forehead.

Colour rules from round 2 still apply: cream = English, butter = backwards sound, sky = rewound or normal sound. There is no reversed or mirrored spelling anywhere.

## Short screens
When the art box is under 200px tall, a container query swaps in a compact 340×120 strip of four mini panels.

## Compromises
- **Compact panel 4** shows two beans and two guesses ("tickle car?" → "nick's cage!") and drops "nickel bar?". Three didn't fit legibly at 120px tall.
- **Compact labels** are shorter: pick / copy backwards / rewind / guess. Text there is about 9–10px, the smallest in the set. It's readable, but it's the limit.
- **Panel 2 needs a small caption** ("your voice, flipped"). Without it, the sky → butter flip could be read as "the app plays something", not "it's *your* voice". It's the only text in the art beyond the labels and example words.
- **Panel 2 shows "your voice" as a waveform**, not a second bean or a speech bubble. That keeps the panel to one glance, but relies on the caption.
- **The 3 → 4 arrow is the one jump in reading order**: down from the right-hand panel to the start of the wide row on the left. The numbers carry it.
- **Panel 3's "pickle jah?" is the near-word** the friend hears. It's deliberately a phonetic misspelling, not reversed letters.
